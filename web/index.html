Lifecycle

Designed around how real estate actually moves.

From acquisition and due diligence to project delivery and long-term operations, the platform keeps data structured as buildings change hands, systems, and states.

Acquire Collect diligence packs, condition data, lease obligations, and risk signals.
Deliver Track BIM handover, commissioning evidence, defect status, and readiness gates.
Operate Manage asset records, preventive workflows, compliance calendars, and vendor performance.
Improve Drive capex prioritization, sustainability upgrades, and portfolio reporting from the same base.
For operations

See what is missing before it becomes a site problem.

Surface incomplete compliance records, missing O&M documentation, repeated failures, and unresolved vendor actions before they roll into reporting cycles.

For leadership

Move from static reports to live portfolio intelligence.

Give investment, asset management, and executive teams a sharper view of operating risk, capex exposure, asset readiness, and portfolio-level trends.
