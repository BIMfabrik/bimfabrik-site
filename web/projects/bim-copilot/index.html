The core problem

BIM often delivers rich information in theory, but teams still struggle to translate it into practical operational workflows. BIM Copilot focuses on that missing handoff.

Strategic role

It serves as a more specialized edge of the BIMfabrik platform, especially valuable during transition from project delivery into steady-state operations.

Connect

Link model data and documents

Preserve relationships between design information, assets, systems, and evidence files.

Review

Measure handover readiness

Highlight missing tags, incomplete records, or package gaps before operations inherits the issue.

Operate

Carry structure forward

Push trusted information into long-term property operations and reporting workflows.
